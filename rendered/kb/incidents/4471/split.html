How the reports split

Placed by tenant and channel with causes.ts, not by subject and not by intake tag.

  • Shared parser. The parser reads every date month first, and every tenant here writes the day first. 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is what #4471 says it is.
  • booking-v3 form hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type month first, as told, and the form reads the value the tenant’s way, day first. It is the same swap with a different cause. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants land here. The same tenants’ app and email reports belong to the parser.
  • One-day move. In these reports day and month do not swap. The date moves by one day: one meeting held on Monday in Toronto and Tuesday in Lisbon, an invite that arrived before its booking. The desk noted them as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) and still filed them under the parser by tenant. A parser fix leaves them as they are. They point to a time zone or daylight saving conversion.

Why one ticket hid them

  • Subject lines do not separate causes. Most subjects in #4471 appear under more than one problem, so merging on subject (decision 0001) puts them all in one ticket.
  • The intake tag guesses from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant. The intake tagged as column above shows how often the two disagree.
  • The ticket-system export repeats rows across pages. Count by report id.
